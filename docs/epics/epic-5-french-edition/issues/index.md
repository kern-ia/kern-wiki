# Issues — Epic 5: French edition

Numbered in dependency order. Issues 01–02 are the mechanism — the second language and the generated
stub that makes a permanently partial tree legible instead of broken. 03–04 are the checks that make
*gaps yes, contradictions no* mechanical rather than aspirational, and they land before the first
translation so that translation is checked by the rules rather than retrofitted to them. 05–08 are
the four front-door translations, one page set per pull request, with 08 conditional on the
conventions page having settled. 09 discharges the epic's single acceptance criterion by reading the
French site rather than asserting it works.

Nothing here translates a per-brick technical page: those are excluded on purpose while the packages
move, and their French URLs serve stubs.
